About Us

Debate Network Nepal (DNN) is the national debate organization of Nepal, dedicated to institutionalizing debating culture since 2013. Registered as a profit-not-distributing company in 2015, DNN is a tax-exempt, youth-led, and non-partisan institution.

Kosedhunga is a flagship program by DNN, focusing on youth empowerment through skill development courses. It offers a 15-day-long skill development course designed to nurture critical thinking, leadership, and communication skills among young individuals.
